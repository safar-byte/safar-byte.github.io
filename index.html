RAG: From Scratch to Production

Course and working service, 2026

Thirteen lessons that take a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline from "embed, search, stuff into a prompt" to hybrid retrieval, reranking, contextual chunking, agentic correction loops and grounded citations.

  • The evaluation harness comes before any optimisation, and every technique after it has to earn its place with a measured delta — including the ones that didn't help.
  • A retrieval inspector shows which stage surfaced each chunk and what it scored.
  • Runs fully offline on local models; Claude and Voyage are one config flag away.

PROBE

Hackathon entry, Forge the Future 2026

Autonomous incident triage for microservices. PROBE watches OpenTelemetry data in Elastic, flags anomalous services, asks Claude on Amazon Bedrock for the single root cause, matches a runbook and opens a Kibana Case — no human in the loop.

  • Detector, Correlator and Remediator as separate FastAPI services.
  • A self-grading harness injects real faults into the OpenTelemetry Astronomy Shop and times how long PROBE takes to name the culprit.

Python, FastAPI, Elasticsearch ES|QL, Amazon Bedrock, Jina embeddings, Docker
